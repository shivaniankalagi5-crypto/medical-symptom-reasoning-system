AI-Based Symptom Analysis & Pattern Reasoning

An educational clinical decision support prototype combining natural language processing, TF-IDF semantic similarity, and weighted knowledge-based inference to provide transparent, explainable symptom pattern matching.

Start Symptom Check View AI Architecture
Important Educational Notice: This system is an educational and informational prototype. It does not provide medical diagnosis, prescribe drugs, or replace a doctor. For medical concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
01

AI Symptom Analysis

Calculates transparent Symptom Pattern Match Scores against evidence-grounded condition profiles using weighted knowledge representations.

02

Natural Language Processing

Processes free-form conversational text, identifies colloquial descriptions, handles negations, and normalizes synonyms into canonical medical entities.

03

Explainable AI (XAI)

Eliminates black-box opacity by showing why results were reached: matched symptoms, missing features, and individual mathematical weight contributions.

04

Risk & Warning-Sign Assessment

Detects predefined critical red-flag emergency symptoms (such as acute chest pain, syncope, or cyanosis) and prompts immediate medical evaluation.
