About the AI System & Methodology

College Artificial Intelligence Mini-Project Documentation.

1. Project Overview & Problem Formulation

The Medical Symptom Reasoning System is an educational prototype developed to explore how hybrid artificial intelligence can bring transparency and safety to digital clinical decision support. Rather than relying on opaque deep neural networks ("black-box" systems) or rigid, hardcoded conditional branches, this system combines natural language processing (NLP), mathematical TF-IDF semantic vectorization, and an evidence-weighted clinical knowledge base.

2. Core AI Technologies & Architecture

Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Clause segmentation, contraction parsing, negation cues detection (e.g., "no fever"), and n-gram phrase matching.

Symptom Normalization

Maps colloquial phrasing (e.g., "head hurts" → Headache, "throwing up" → Vomiting) to canonical medical IDs.

TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity

Converts patient narratives and pattern profiles into term-frequency/inverse-document-frequency vectors to calculate semantic angles.

Weighted Knowledge-Based Reasoning

Evaluates symptom coverage against condition patterns using clinically grounded weight factors ($w \in [0.10, 0.95]$).

Explainable AI (XAI)

Transparently breaks down why results were generated: matched symptoms, weights, missing symptoms, and mathematical contribution.

Rule-Based Red Flag Assessment

Instantly flags critical emergency signs (e.g., chest pain, syncope, cyanosis) to urge immediate emergency care.

3. Transparent Scoring Mathematics

The system computes a calibrated Symptom Pattern Match Score:

$$\text{Coverage}(P) = \frac{\sum_{s \in S_{\text{matched}}} w(s, P)}{\sum_{s \in S_P} w(s, P)}$$
$$\text{Pattern Match Score} = \left[0.75 \times \text{Coverage}(P) + 0.25 \times \text{CosineSimilarity}(Q, P)\right] \times 100$$

Crucial Note: The score is strictly titled a Symptom Pattern Match Score. It does NOT represent a probability of disease, diagnosis confidence, or clinical prognosis.

4. Medical Safety & Academic Disclaimer

This software is built solely for educational and coursework demonstration purposes. It does NOT prescribe medication, recommend drug dosages, or diagnose illnesses. Anyone experiencing persistent, severe, or concerning symptoms should seek immediate care from a qualified healthcare practitioner.
